When to call before it gets worse

Most Florida AC failures give a warning first. Catching it early is usually the cheaper fix.

  • 1
    Air is blowing, but not coldOften a capacitor or low refrigerant.
  • 2
    Water near the indoor unitUsually a clogged drain line. Turn the system off.
  • 3
    Clicking, humming or buzzingElectrical parts wearing out. Worth a look this week.
  • 4
    Power bill jumpedDirty coils or a struggling motor working overtime.
